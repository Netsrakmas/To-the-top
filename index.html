A QUICK SWIMMING LESSON

Small fish. Smart moves.

01

Keep moving up.Hold anywhere in the water to swim. Drag left or right to steer. Lift to slow down. On a keyboard, hold Space / ↑ / W and steer with ← → or A D.

02

Follow the bubbles.Collect the bright rings. Dodge creatures; rocks block your path. Cyan shields absorb a hit. Yellow bolts give you a burst of speed.

03

Turn small into mighty.Swim through all three nets to trap your hunters, then reach the surface. Watch the warning at the bottom: the hunter is getting close.

Need a gentler start? Choose Easy tide, or enable Auto-swim in Settings. Pause any time with Esc or P.
